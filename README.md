# COHH - Clock Of Human History


## Shape
- `circler` just like regular clock
- `10` numbers other then 12 numbers
- `3` hands or `2` hands just like regular clock
- top numer is `0` not 10 or 12
- bottom numer is `5` not 6


## Hands

Logicly and functionaly they are pertty same with regular clock


### hand100

- this is equivalent of hour-hand in regular clock
- one step of it means 100 years past
- full 10 steps mean 1000 years past


### hand10

- this is equivalent of minute-hand in regular clock
- one step of it means 10 years past
- full 10 steps mean 100 years past


### hand1

- this is equivalent of second-hand in regular clock
- one step of it means 1 years past
- full 10 steps mean 10 years past
